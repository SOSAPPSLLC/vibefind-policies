vibefind — Child Safety Policy

Last updated: October 2026

vibefind is committed to providing a safe experience for all users 
and maintaining zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation 
(CSAE) in any form.

APP DESIGN AND CHILD SAFETY

vibefind is designed with child safety as a core principle:

- The app does not contain any chat, messaging, or direct 
  communication features between users
- Users are anonymous to each other — only a first name / user created "handle" and 
  shared interests are ever visible
- The app includes an optional age verification toggle that 
  allows users to filter so they only see other confirmed 
  adult users
- No user-generated content, images, or media can be shared 
  through the app
- No personal data is stored on any server — all matching 
  happens locally on the device via Bluetooth

ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY

vibefind has zero tolerance for the use of our platform to 
exploit, abuse, or endanger children in any way. Any use of 
the app to facilitate contact with or exploitation of minors 
is strictly prohibited and will be reported to the 
appropriate authorities.

REPORTING

If you become aware of any activity on vibefind that 
endangers a child, please contact us immediately at:
ABUSE@smallofficesystems.biz

We will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations 
relating to child safety.

ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in immediate removal 
from the app and reporting to the National Center for 
Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or equivalent 
authority in the relevant jurisdiction.
